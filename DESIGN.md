# Design System: XIII Renovation & Design

## Creative north star

The website reads like a beautifully edited residential design folio: warm paper, large ink serif headlines, compact sans-serif supporting copy, and completed XIII projects arranged as an architectural composition. The visual structure follows the supplied LumiNest reference while every claim, photograph, review, and contact path remains XIII’s own.

## Colour

- Warm off-white is the main canvas; near-white paper distinguishes content surfaces.
- Near-black ink carries headings and active service or FAQ panels.
- XIII amber is used for calls to action, tiny wayfinding marks, review stars, and linework. It is never a large background field.
- Body text uses warm grey with at least readable body contrast on the canvas.

## Typography

- Playfair Display speaks for page and section headlines, card titles, and testimonial quotations.
- Outfit carries navigation, body copy, labels, and controls.
- Hero display type ranges up to 6rem with tight but never tighter than -0.04em tracking.
- Copy stays concise and generally within 65–75 characters per line.

## Composition

- The content column is 1200px with 1.5rem desktop and 1rem mobile minimum gutters.
- Major sections use 8.5rem desktop and 6rem mobile vertical spacing.
- The hero is photographic, left-aligned, and nearly full viewport height.
- Services use a compact editorial introduction opposite a numbered vertical stack.
- Project inspiration uses an irregular six-image mosaic.
- Proof cards stagger vertically on desktop and become a clean single column on mobile.
- Contact begins in the project-type cards and completes through WhatsApp, phone, or email.

## Surfaces and controls

- Cards use 13–14px radii; internal images use 9–10px radii.
- Use either a warm hairline or a soft downward shadow, not both.
- Buttons are compact warm-amber rectangles with 5–6px corners and plain-language action labels.
- Focus rings, selection, scrollbars, and review controls use XIII amber.

## Motion

- The authored entrance is the hero: a slow photographic settle paired with clipped copy arrival.
- The vertical scroll cue runs continuously but disappears on mobile.
- Content reveals are restrained and staggered by group; reduced-motion preference disables them.
- Project imagery gently scales only on intentional hover.
- Review navigation is manual; no autonomous carousel movement.

## Content rules

- Show completed XIII work rather than fallback or stock imagery.
- Do not invent prices, guarantees, awards, project counts, or testimonials.
- Cost-related calls to action start a scope conversation instead of implying a calculator that does not exist.
- Public identity is always XIII Renovation & Design; UEN and contact details remain exact.
