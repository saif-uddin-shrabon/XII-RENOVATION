## 2. Key findings

- **tiler singapore** (1,000/mo, SGD 0.95–5.98) is the largest tiling term, and it is cheap for its volume. **tiling contractor singapore** (210/mo, SGD 3.30–12.60) has the strongest commercial signal.
- **epoxy grout singapore** (480/mo) is surprisingly large. Much of it is product-buying intent (people shopping for grout), so validate it with a small test before putting it in the launch campaign.
- **Repair intent** is solid and lower-competition: tile repair singapore (140/mo), fix hollow tiles, popping tiles, cracked tile. These jobs are often small but lead to larger retiling projects.
- **Overlay** terms are small but very specific (tile overlay singapore, overlay toilet tiles singapore — 10–30/mo each). Overlay is a common HDB/condo toilet upgrade that avoids hacking.
- Many CSV rows are tile **products** ("mosaic tiles singapore", "terrazzo tiles singapore", "hafary tiles singapore"). Those searchers are shopping for tiles in a showroom, not hiring a tiler. They are marked retail and kept for SEO ideas only.
- The big generic rows (bathroom tiling 390, kitchen tiling 320, kitchen backsplash 480) have no geo modifier and lean DIY/design. Bid on them only with Singapore location targeting and phrase match.
- HDB note: tiling inside HDB flats is renovation work covered by the DRC rule (see the HDB file).
