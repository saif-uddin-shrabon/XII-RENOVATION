## 2. Key findings

- The single biggest commercial head term in the whole dataset is **kitchen cabinet singapore / kitchen cabinet sg / kitchen cupboard singapore** (5,000/mo each — Keyword Planner groups these as one close-variant cluster, so treat it as ~5,000 searches, not 15,000). CPC SGD 1.43–4.05 is cheap for its size.
- **kitchen renovation singapore** (720/mo, High comp, SGD 1.88–8.75) and **kitchen renovation contractor** (90/mo, SGD 5.63–23.26) carry the highest-value renovation intent. Expect the contractor variant to produce the best leads per click.
- A large **cabinet door / front replacement** cluster (90/mo grouped) exists, but most phrasings are UK/US-style ("kitchen unit doors", "reface"). Useful as an SEO section and a low-bid P3 ad group, not a launch priority.
- **Aluminium / stainless steel kitchen cabinet singapore** (500/mo grouped) is a material-specific market served by specialist aluminium fabricators. Bid on it only if XIII actually builds aluminium carcasses; otherwise add it as a negative so the budget is not spent on clicks that will not convert.
- **HDB kitchen design** searches (kitchen hdb design 390, 4 room hdb kitchen design 320, bto 4 room kitchen design 140) are design-research intent. They are cheap (SGD ~1.0–2.7) and good for SEO galleries and top-of-funnel ads. The contractor terms still convert better.
- Appliance installation (hob, hood, dishwasher) is a long tail of 10–70/mo terms, mostly from people who need one appliance swapped. It fits as a section on the kitchen page but should not run as a standalone ad group.
- Backsplash/countertop terms are dominated by design-browsing and DIY queries ("glass backsplash", colour combinations). Only the Singapore-modified ones (kitchen backsplash singapore 90/mo) are worth targeting.
