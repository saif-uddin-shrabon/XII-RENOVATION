## 2. Key findings

- Search demand is **small**. Wall hacking singapore (50/mo), hacking contractor singapore (30/mo) and hacking floor tiles cost singapore (30/mo) are the largest core hacking terms. The total of clean commercial demand after removing grouped duplicates is under 1,000/mo.
- **demolition contractor/company singapore** (210/mo grouped, SGD 1.64–5.16) is the largest term, but it attracts commercial and industrial demolition buyers as well as homeowners.
- **Reinstatement** (reinstatement contractor singapore 140/mo, SGD 8.05–26.08; office reinstatement singapore 90/mo, SGD 9.66–18.50) has the **highest CPC in this category**. It is commercial (office/shop lease-end) work. It suits the overview's "commercial spaces" line but not the homeowner-first positioning in PRODUCT.md.
- **Furniture / bulky disposal** (bulky item disposal hdb 390/mo, furniture disposal 260/mo) is a different business (disposal/rag-and-bone). It is excluded from ads, and only "debris disposal included" is kept as a selling point.
- Most hacking demand is captured **upstream** by searchers looking for resale HDB renovations and kitchen or toilet renovations. Hacking works best as a supporting page and ad group, not a lead campaign.
- HDB rule context (for content): hacking needs an HDB permit, and noisy works are restricted to weekday hours. Write these as how-it-works explanations, not as compliance claims.
