## 2. Key findings

- **plumber sg** (5,400/mo, SGD 5.02–13.36) is the **largest keyword in the entire dataset**, and plumber service singapore (1,900/mo grouped) is next. Plumbing has the highest raw demand of any category.
- However, the demand is dominated by **urgent repair intent** (leaks, choked toilets, 24-hour and emergency plumbers) with high CPCs (SGD 13–14 high range). XIII is a renovation contractor, not a rapid-response plumbing service, so most of these clicks would not become renovation projects.
- Renovation-aligned subsets do exist: **toilet bowl replacement** (170/mo; hdb toilet bowl replacement cost 110/mo), **water heater replacement** (170/mo grouped) and **changing toilet bowl singapore** (170/mo). These are best handled as sanitary-fitting sections inside the kitchen and bathroom renovation scope.
- Hyper-local plumber terms (plumber tampines 140, plumber yishun 110, **plumber sengkang 90**, plumber punggol 70) show that local searching is strong. Sengkang/Punggol is XIII's home area.
- About 900 of the ~1,900 relevant rows are DIY or US-style problem queries ("toilet bowl flapper replacement", "rough in plumbing"). They are kept only as blog/FAQ material.
- Verdict: **not a launch category**. Revisit after the LP arrangement is confirmed and the business decides whether it wants small, urgent repair jobs.

**Column guide**
- *Avg. monthly searches, Competition, bids, 3-mo / YoY*: copied verbatim from Google Keyword Planner CSVs (currency SGD). "—" = blank in source.
- **↺** = this row has metrics identical to a higher-volume row in the same table. Keyword Planner groups close variants, so ↺ rows share **one** demand pool and should **not** be added together.
- *Intent / Lead quality / Google Ads / SEO / Landing*: analyst classification (rule-based, then reviewed). It is **not** Google data.
- **Google Ads priority**: **P1** = launch ad group; **P2** = launch at lower bids or add in weeks 2–4; **P3** = long-tail / later expansion (often caught by phrase match anyway); **Hold** = only after the business confirms an unverified claim (e.g. HDB DRC listing); **No** = do not bid; **Negative** = add as negative keyword.
