## 2. Key findings

- **hdb renovation contractor / hdb reno contractor / hdb contractors for renovation** (480/mo grouped, Medium competition, index 47, SGD 2.97–9.18) is the anchor. Competition is **Medium**, unusually low for such a high-value term, which makes it one of the best value-for-money terms in the dataset.
- **hdb renovation singapore** (140/mo) has the highest top-of-page bid of the core terms (SGD 3.17–12.78). That signals advertisers see strong conversion value.
- **Compliance terms**: "hdb approved / licensed / registered / accredited contractor" (880/mo grouped) and "hdb approved renovation contractor" (390/mo grouped). HDB requires flat owners to use a contractor listed in its **Directory of Renovation Contractors (DRC)**. A non-listed firm may not advertise itself as listed. These terms are marked **Hold** until XIII confirms its DRC listing. After that, they are among the best qualified terms available.
- **BTO** (4 room bto renovation 170/mo, SGD 1.81–4.09) and **resale/old flat** (old hdb 4 room renovation 140/mo; old hdb 5 room renovation design 140/mo) are separate customer journeys. Resale renovations involve hacking, rewiring and waterproofing (higher ticket). BTO renovations are mostly carpentry plus finishes.
- Room-size modifiers (3/4/5-room) are everywhere. "4 room" dominates, which matches Singapore's most common flat type.
- The CSVs were seeded only with HDB terms. Broad high-volume terms such as **home renovation singapore, renovation contractor singapore, interior design singapore, condo renovation, landed renovation** do **not appear in any CSV**. This is the biggest research gap for the category and should be pulled before the campaign goes beyond HDB.
- Toilet/bathroom renovation appears only weakly (4 room hdb toilet renovation 90/mo; singapore hdb toilet renovation 40/mo). "bathroom renovation singapore" itself was never seeded, which is another gap.
