## 2. Key findings

- **carpentry sg** (2,900/mo, SGD 2.64–8.93) and **carpentry singapore / carpenter sg** (2,400/mo grouped, SGD 2.25–8.28) are the two largest service head terms of any category except plumbing.
- **Direct / factory carpentry** (direct carpentry singapore 500/mo, SGD 3.43–10.23) is a strong price-sensitive segment. These buyers want factory pricing without an interior designer. That matches XIII's message of one contractor with no middle-man markup, but these leads will be price-driven.
- **Built-in / custom wardrobe** (custom wardrobe singapore 260/mo; built in wardrobe singapore 210/mo; SGD 1.46–4.89) is the highest-intent product line, with clear quoting units (per foot run).
- **Shoe cabinet / storage cabinet singapore** (1,900/mo and 320/mo) are mostly **ready-made furniture shoppers**. Ready-made furniture sellers and IKEA take most of that traffic. They are classified as retail and are not for launch ads. Use "custom/built-in shoe cabinet" phrasing instead.
- "carpenternear me" (500/mo, a typo variant Keyword Planner returned) shows that "near me" demand exists. Locally-intended searches are best captured with location targeting plus a Google Business Profile, not with exact "near me" keywords.
- Price research (carpentry price singapore 40/mo, carpentry cost singapore 50/mo, built in wardrobe singapore price/cost 50/mo) is moderate. A transparent per-foot-run price guide on the page would capture it organically.
- Low-quality modifiers to filter: freelance, handyman, small/simple carpentry work, malaysia/JB carpenter, cheap.
