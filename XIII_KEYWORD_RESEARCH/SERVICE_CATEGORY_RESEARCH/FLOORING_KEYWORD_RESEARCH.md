## 2. Key findings

- **vinyl tiles singapore / vinyl tile flooring singapore** (2,900/mo grouped, SGD 2.36–9.28) is the dominant flooring demand and the largest non-plumbing service-adjacent cluster in the data. Vinyl installation sits close to XIII's tiling work.
- **Marble polishing** (390/mo; marble floor polishing 320/mo; SGD ~2.9–8.8) is a specialist restoration service, common in landed homes and condos.
- **Epoxy flooring** (epoxyflooring 2,900/mo, epoxy floor paint 480/mo) is largely DIY-paint and industrial intent. It is unsuitable for a residential-premium brand unless XIII offers epoxy toilet/kitchen coatings.
- **Carpet** demand is product/retail and office oriented. It is not a fit.
- **Screed** terms (self leveling screed 170/mo) are product queries. Screeding is better presented as a step within tiling or flooring than as a page of its own.
- Recommendation: put a vinyl/SPC flooring section on the tiling page or create a dedicated /vinyl-flooring page. Exclude epoxy and carpet from launch ads.
