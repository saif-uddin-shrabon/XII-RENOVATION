## 2. Key findings

- Raw volume is large (partition wall 1,600/mo; room dividers 1,000/mo), but **almost none of it is Singapore-modified or contractor-intent**. Most of it is people shopping for movable room dividers and screens (IKEA and furniture retailers).
- **Office partition** (210/mo, SGD 1.84–7.07) and glass/meeting-room partition terms are commercial fit-out intent. They are relevant only if XIII pursues commercial clients.
- **Drywall/gypsum partition wall** (drywall partition wall 170/mo grouped; **Low** competition, no bid data, YoY +600%) is the true contractor term and is growing. Its phrasing is generic, so it needs SG location targeting.
- **False ceiling** has almost no CSV coverage. The data contains removal and repair terms but none of the core "false ceiling singapore" family. This is a **major data gap**: false ceilings with cove lighting are very common in Singapore renovations.
- Moisture-resistant board and pipe box-up terms (waterproof plasterboard 320/mo) are mostly product queries. Box-up work is a useful item for SEO FAQs.
- Verdict: not a launch category on current data. Pull Singapore-specific "false ceiling" and "partition wall singapore" data first.

**Column guide**
- *Avg. monthly searches, Competition, bids, 3-mo / YoY*: copied verbatim from Google Keyword Planner CSVs (currency SGD). "—" = blank in source.
