## 2. Key findings

- Waterproofing has the **highest CPCs of any category**: waterproofing singapore (260/mo, SGD 6.07–22.38), waterproofing contractor singapore (140/mo, SGD 10.10–24.09), roof leakage repair (140/mo grouped, SGD 7.25–20.17). Leak problems are urgent and specialists compete hard for them.
- **Toilet waterproofing** (140/mo; waterproofing toilet singapore 50/mo; hdb toilet waterproofing 20/mo) aligns most closely with XIII's bathroom tiling and overlay work.
- **Ceiling leak / water seepage** terms are high-intent but come with diagnosis and liability complexity. HDB rules on inter-floor leaks split responsibility between neighbours, and HDB has tested non-hacking membrane systems. Lead quality is high, but many enquiries are diagnostic jobs.
- Roof waterproofing and PU injection belong to the landed and commercial specialist market. They are low fit for launch.
- Verdict: strong margin potential but no landing page and specialist competition. It is a good **phase-2** category to launch alongside a toilet waterproofing + retiling offer.
